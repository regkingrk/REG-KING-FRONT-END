## Show plates: own spacing and custom lengths (added 13 Nov 2026)
- The tap-a-gap "Character spacing" section is gone. On show plates (cars) customers space the reg in the reg box as they like (up to 3 spaces between characters). Road-legal and bike plates keep the usual spacing.
- New "Custom" size tab on show plates (cars): any length, rectangle or hex, 111mm tall. The shortest offered is the shortest the reg fits at full size with 11mm to each end, so letters never overhang.
